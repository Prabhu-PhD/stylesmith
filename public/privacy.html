StyleSmith Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 July 2026

StyleSmith is a Microsoft PowerPoint add-in published by Toss The Coin. This policy explains what the add-in does and does not do with your data.

What data StyleSmith stores

StyleSmith stores your style and token definitions inside your PowerPoint file, using the document's own settings store and per-shape tags. This data travels with the .pptx and never leaves your device or your file through StyleSmith.

What StyleSmith does not do

  • It does not send your document, its contents, or any style data to any server. There is no StyleSmith backend.
  • It does not collect personal information, analytics, or telemetry.
  • It does not use cookies or trackers.
  • It does not read or transmit content beyond the shape formatting needed to create and apply styles within the open document.

Permissions

The add-in requests ReadWriteDocument permission so it can read shape formatting and apply styles you create. This access is scoped to the presentation you have open.

Third parties

The add-in's code is served as static files. The Office JavaScript runtime is loaded from Microsoft's official CDN, subject to Microsoft's own policies.
